[h1]Stackland Access – play Stacklands with a screen reader[/h1]

Stackland Access makes Stacklands fully playable for blind and visually impaired players, with the keyboard or a controller and your screen reader (NVDA, JAWS and others through Tolk, with the Windows voice SAPI as a fallback).

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Menus:[/b] every menu, option, slider, dialog, popup and cutscene is read aloud.
[*][b]Board:[/b] browse everything by category (people, food, resources, buildings, enemies, animals, equipment, ideas, locations, shop…) with one-key shortcuts.
[*][b]Cards:[/b] pick up with Enter and drop on a stack, the sell box, a pack to buy or a travelling merchant. Coins are paid automatically.
[*][b]Details (I):[/b] sell price, health, combat stats, effects, current action, description, villager inventory and equipment.
[*][b]Recipes (L):[/b] what you can make right now, what each production building can make (with a list of choices), and every idea, including recipes you found without the idea. Enter sets the recipe up for you.
[*][b]Connections (E):[/b] energy, sewer and transport links of a stack, and where the linked cards are.
[*][b]Announcements:[/b] new cards, enemies, combat hits with remaining health, recipes starting and finishing, quests, end of moon warning, status effects.
[*][b]Villagers:[/b] a short "ready" chime when a villager becomes free; Ctrl+P lists what every villager is doing.
[*][b]Comfort:[/b] automatic stacking of identical idle resources, F5 to regroup everything, game speed with - / + or the numpad, move stacks with Ctrl+Enter.
[*][b]Options (F7):[/b] turn each feature on or off and change every key. Controller support, with every action on the buttons.
[*][b]On screen:[/b] the mod's menus are also shown in the game's style for the people watching, with clickable buttons. A sighted player can turn the narration off at first launch (Ctrl+F2 to switch).
[*][b]Card list (F6):[/b] a web page with every card, idea and pack of the game: recipes, pack odds, drops, villager and enemy stats, filters and search.
[*][b]Languages:[/b] English, French, Dutch, German, Italian, Spanish, Polish, Brazilian Portuguese, Japanese, Korean, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional). Languages other than English and French were translated with AI, corrections are welcome.
[/list]

[h2]Getting started[/h2]
Subscribe, start the game, and you will hear "Stackland Access ready". Press [b]F1[/b] at any time for the list of keys, [b]F2[/b] to repeat the last message.
Press [b]F7[/b] for the options (also in Options > Mods > Stackland Access).
Tip for NVDA users: keep NumLock on to use the numpad speed keys.

[h2]Stackland Access 0.7.0[/h2]
[b]New[/b]
[list]
[*][b]F7, options:[/b] turn each feature on or off and change every key. A key already used goes to the other action.
[*][b]Controller support:[/b] the whole mod works with a controller. Hold LT or RT for more actions. F1 lists the buttons. The game's own controller controls are turned off while it is on (option).
[*][b]Menus on screen:[/b] the mod's menus are also drawn in the game's style for the people watching, with clickable buttons, without pausing the game. Villagers top right, recipes bottom right, help top left.
[*][b]First launch:[/b] you choose whether the narration is on. "No" makes the mod completely silent, for a sighted player; Ctrl+F2 switches it at any time.
[*][b]What's new:[/b] after an update, a window lists the changes once (also the first line of the help, F1).
[*][b]E, connections:[/b] energy, sewer and transport links of a stack, what they give it and where the linked cards are; connect and disconnect from the list.
[*][b]F6, card list:[/b] a web page with every card, idea and pack of the game, made from your game and language: recipes, pack odds, drops, filters (type, content, tech age, how to get it), and tables of villager and enemy stats (health, attack, defence, attack speed, hit chance, special effects). Shift+F6 makes it as a public website in every language.
[/list]
[b]Improved[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Recipes (L):[/b] the production page only offers what a building can really make now with your cards. With several choices (a stove: omelette, quiche…), Enter opens the list of choices.
[*]Recipes you made without having the idea are now known, in L and on the card list.
[*]"All ideas and rumors" page back in L; rumors are listed in their group.
[*][b]F5[/b] regroups every identical idle card, also out of mixed piles; [b]Ctrl+F5[/b] only the current category.
[*]Production buildings are always in the Buildings category (H). The Industrial Revolution card is announced and explained.
[/list]
